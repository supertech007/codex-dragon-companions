# Moki look mechanics

- Moki is a plush otter with forward-set amber eyes behind small round glasses, a centered black nose, subtle whisker pads, rounded ears, teal scarf, and a small slate indicator pad held at mid-torso.
- Turn gaze by shifting both pupils together within the existing eye/glasses openings. Keep eye size and glasses rims unchanged.
- For screen-right/left, turn the muzzle and whisker pads slightly toward the target while preserving the round head silhouette; the nose should visibly move toward the target edge.
- For up/down, keep the face broadly frontal and move both pupils toward the top/bottom of the eye openings; use a slight muzzle pitch and ear/head posture without rotating the whole body.
- Keep scarf and torso compact. Preserve the pad as a small attached identity prop; its two five-lamp rows remain on the front of the pad.
- All sixteen poses should read as gaze changes of the same pet, with no added symbols or detached effects.
